Gumball Watterson

Species: Cat

Age: 12

Role: Main Character

First Appearance: Season 1, Episode 1

Backstory

Gumball is a mischievous 12-year-old cat who lives in Elmore with his family. He is always coming up with big plans that usually end in chaos, but he means well and cares deeply about his family and friends.

Personality Traits

Impulsive, funny, loyal, and a bit lazy when it comes to schoolwork.
